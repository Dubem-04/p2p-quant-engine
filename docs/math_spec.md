# Mathematical Formulations & Quantitative Specifications

### System Scope:
Documents the theoretical derivations, continuous-time formulas, and discrete
algorithmic equations used throughout the engine:
1. **Net Round-Trip Margin**: Complete fee breakdown factoring multi-tier platform and bank friction.
2. **Capital Turnover & Cycle Velocity**: Formal calculations for capital velocity and periodic returns.
3. **Avellaneda-Stoikov P2P Adaptation**: Inventory reservation price and asymmetric quoting equations.
4. **Risk-Adjusted Return on Capital (RAROC)**: Equations penalizing high-risk settlement counterparties.
