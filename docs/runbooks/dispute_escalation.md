# Incident Response Runbook: Dispute Escalation & Fraud Handling

### Operational Scope:
Defines defensive protocols for disputed orders, bank holds, or non-responsive counterparties:
1. **Third-Party Payment Flagging**: Exact legal and platform appeal scripts when sender name mismatches.
2. **Bank Lien / PND Immediate Actions**: Emergency account isolation procedures to protect operational capital.
3. **Evidence Compiling**: Automated generation of bank statements and timestamps for exchange moderators.
